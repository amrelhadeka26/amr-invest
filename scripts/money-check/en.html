The 5-minute
money check.

Five questions, a pen and your last month's numbers. No finance degree needed. You'll see where your money stands before you invest a single pound.

1

Where does your money go?

Write your monthly income and what you spent last month. Take spending away from income: that's what you keep. Divide it by income to get your savings rate.

I keep each month
Savings rate
2

How long would your savings last?

Divide the cash you can reach quickly by your monthly spending. That's how many months you could cover if income stopped. Many planners use 3 to 6 months as a common reference point.

Months covered
3

How much goes to debt?

Add up all monthly installments and loan payments. Divide by income. For every installment plan, ask for the real yearly rate, not the "flat" rate. Upfront fees count too.

Debt payments ÷ income
4

Is your cash losing to prices?

How much of what you own sits as cash in pounds, earning nothing? Prices rose about 13.9% in the year to September 2026 (CAPMAS), so cash that earns nothing buys less each year.

Cash earning nothing
% of what I own
5

Is the app licensed?

Before you deposit anywhere: find the company's full legal name, search it at fra.gov.eg, check the licence covers what you're buying. Vague answers? Walk away.
