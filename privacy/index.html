Privacy notice and terms

For ZD AI (invest.amrelhadeka.com/zd) and invest.amrelhadeka.com. Last updated 9 November 2026.

Who we are

ZD AI and this website are run by Amr El Hadeka in Egypt. Questions or requests: invest@amrelhadeka.com.

What we collect

  • If you sign in with Google: your name and email address. We don't get your Google password, contacts or files.
  • If you create an account with email: your email, a name and your password (stored only as a secure hash).
  • What you choose to enter in ZD AI: for example income, spending, savings, debts, goals and the questions you ask. You never need to enter bank account numbers, card numbers or passwords, and ZD AI will never ask for them.
  • Guest accounts: if you try ZD AI without signing in, we create a private guest account just for you. It is deleted automatically after 7 days.
  • Website usage: anonymous daily counts of which pages are visited, the site you came from (for example Instagram or Google), your country and whether you use a phone or a computer. No cookies and no IP addresses are stored for this, and a visitor can't be followed from one day to the next. If your browser sends "Do Not Track" or "Global Privacy Control", the visit isn't counted.
  • Newsletter and sessions: if you subscribe or book a session, your email (and booking details) go to our newsletter tool (Brevo) or booking tool (Calendly).

Why we use it

  • To run ZD AI for you: show your numbers, answer your questions and keep the results you save.
  • To understand, in totals only, how the website and ZD AI are used (for example how many people visit, which tools they open and which topics come up), so we can improve them. These totals never show who said what.
  • To keep the service safe and affordable, for example daily limits on AI answers and limits on new guest accounts.
  • We don't sell your information and we don't use it for advertising.

Who processes it

  • Cloudflare hosts the website and ZD AI. ZD AI's database is stored in Cloudflare's Eastern Europe region.
  • Anthropic (the company behind Claude) processes the questions you ask ZD AI, together with the figures ZD AI needs to answer them, to write each reply. Anthropic does not use this data to train its models.
  • Google, only if you choose "Continue with Google", to confirm who you are.

How long we keep it

  • Chat messages: up to 180 days, then deleted automatically.
  • Guest accounts: deleted after 7 days.
  • Accounts: until you delete them.

Your choices

  • In ZD AI, under Settings, you can download a copy of your data or delete your account and everything in it at any time.
  • You can also email us to ask what we hold about you, to correct it or to delete it.
