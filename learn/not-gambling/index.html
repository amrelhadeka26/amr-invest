Myth 3

“Investing is basically gambling.”

Gambling is built so that, over time, you lose. Long-term, diversified investing is built so that, over time, you own a share of real growth.

By Amr El Hadeka · 2 min read

In 30 seconds

  • Gambling is built so that you lose over time.
  • Investing means owning, or lending to, something that produces value.
  • It turns into gambling when you chase tips, go all in, or invest money you need soon.

Prices go up and down, some people lose, and there’s always someone with a “sure tip.” From outside it can look like a casino. But the two work in opposite ways.

Gambling: the odds are against you

Every casino game is built with an edge for the house. You might win one night, but the longer you play, the more certain it is that you lose. Nothing is created; money just moves to the house.

Investing: you own something real

A share is a small piece of a real business and its profits. A certificate is a loan that pays you interest. Over long periods businesses grow, and investors share in that. Time works for you.

When investing becomes gambling

  • All your money in one share because of a tip.
  • Chasing whatever went up most last month.
  • Trading in and out, guessing the next move.
  • Borrowing to invest, or investing money you need soon.

The fix is the opposite of each: spread out, think in years, keep costs low, and invest only money that can wait.

Gambling is betting on what happens next. Investing is owning a share of what grows over time.
