Myth 1

“I need a lot of money to start.”

You don’t. Starting small and starting early beats waiting for a big amount that may never come.

By Amr El Hadeka · 2 min read

In 30 seconds

  • You don’t need a big amount. You need to start.
  • Time does more work than the amount.
  • Begin with what you won’t miss, and make it automatic.

“When I have 100,000 pounds, then I’ll start.” That day rarely comes. Money without a job tends to get spent.

Time does more work than the amount

When your returns start earning returns of their own, growth speeds up. The key ingredient is time, not the size of your first deposit.

Same 1,000 pounds a month. The only difference is time. Try your own numbers in grow.

What starting small looks like

  • Keep a small safety cushion first, so an emergency doesn’t force you to sell.
  • Pick an amount you won’t miss. Even 500 or 1,000 pounds a month is a real start.
  • Move it on payday, before you spend, ideally automatically.
  • Raise it when your income grows.

Your first year won’t make you rich. It gives you the habit, and the habit is the real asset.

Don’t wait until you have a lot of money to start. Start, and let time help you build it.
