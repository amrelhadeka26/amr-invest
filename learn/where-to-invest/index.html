What you can invest in · Where can you invest?

Every investment has a job, and a risk.

Seven places your money can go. Understanding the difference between them matters more than chasing the highest return.

By Amr El Hadeka · 3 min read

In 30 seconds

  • Lending (cash, certificates, bonds) is steady. Owning (stocks, funds, gold, property) can grow more, and swings more.
  • Every option has a job, a risk and a cost.
  • Choose by when you’ll need the money.

I won’t tell you what to buy. Here’s what each option is, how it earns and what can go wrong.

Lending: cash and fixed income

Cash (savings accounts, certificates) earns interest and is easy to reach: the right home for your emergency fund. Over long periods it can trail inflation. Fixed income means lending to the government or companies through T-bills, bonds or sukuk, often via a money market or fixed-income fund. Low risk, not zero.

Owning: stocks and funds

Stocks make you part-owner of a company, in Egypt or abroad. They can grow a lot and fall hard. Equity and index funds spread your money across many companies at once. Watch the yearly fee: one extra percent can cost tens of thousands of pounds over 20 years.

Owning things: gold and property

Gold earns only from its price. It has protected many Egyptians when the pound weakened, but it pays no income and can go flat for years. Real estate earns from rent and price growth, but a whole flat is a big ticket and slow to sell. Fractional apps let you own a share from a few thousand pounds.

Beyond borders

The pound went from about 8 to over 50 per dollar in roughly a decade, which is why many Egyptians want some dollar exposure. Currency moves both ways, and transfers have limits and fees. Thndr now gives Egyptian residents access to US stocks.

Match the tool to the goal

Need it in six months? Safety first. A home in five years? A balance, with more safety as the date gets close. Wealth over twenty years? Growth and low fees matter most. Your answer depends on your situation.

Different investments do different jobs. Know the job, the risk and the cost before the return.
