Where the numbers come from

Last 10 years uses what actually happened: EGX30 rose 12.8% a year on price alone from end-2014 to end-2024, plus dividends (EGX30 yearly returns). The S&P 500 returned 14.8% a year in dollars, 2016–2025 (Fidelity). 21K gold went from 287.62 EGP a gram in April 2013 (Amwal Al Ghad) to about 6,217 today (GoldPriceZ), about 25.6% a year in pounds: roughly 7.6% for gold in dollars and 16.7% for the pound's fall. Property rose about 12.2% a year in the Aqarmap index, 2014–2024, with gross rents of 6.7% (Global Property Guide).

Balanced and Cautious are lower forward-looking guesses, because the last decade included two big devaluations.

Today: inflation 13.9% in September 2026 (Daily News Egypt). 3-year fixed certificates about 17.75% (Enterprise). EGX trades pay 0.05% stamp duty each way since August 2026 (Zawya). Default costs for gold, brokers and property are estimates.

Worst years on record: EGX30 −49.3% (2011), S&P 500 −37% (2008, USD), gold −28% (2013, USD), property −9.6% (2020). Checked 24 October 2026.
